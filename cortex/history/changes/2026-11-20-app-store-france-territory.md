On 2026-11-20, with the owner's permission, the releases lead set the France territory of the App Store listing to available
through the App Store Connect API (PAD-832): 174 of 175 territories were on, France was the one off. The read-back showed
Apple processing the change; the French store page showed the app at 1.3.4 the same day and at 1.3.5 on 2026-11-23. The
listing has no French localisation, so that page shows the Portuguese description. How the territory endpoints work is in
`world/systems/app-store-connect.md`.
