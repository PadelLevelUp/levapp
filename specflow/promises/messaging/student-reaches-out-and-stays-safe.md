# Student reaches out and stays safe

## Outcome

A student can start a conversation with anyone whose exact username they know, with no shared roster or friend request. A message from someone they share no club with arrives clearly marked, with Block and Report one tap away, so an unwanted message is a one-time event, never a channel.

- Anyone may message any active user by exact username: student to student, student to a coach who is not theirs, coach to anyone.
- Nobody is listed or searchable beyond the people you are already connected with; the picker's search only filters that list.
- Coaches keep their reach (their own students and those of classes they teach) and gain none; the players of a club a coach merely belongs to are not part of it (PAD-568).
- A conversation is "unknown" to a viewer when the other person is not linked to them on a roster (in either direction), shares no club with them, and the viewer has never written a text message in it that still stands; the banner shows exactly then.
- A block works both ways and is enforced by the server: neither can start a conversation with, or message, the other. Blocking sends no notice to the blocked person, though a message they try to send is refused.
- A report is made only from inside the reporter's own conversation and always points at a specific message.
- Block and report are available on web and iOS, in the thread, and blocks also in Settings.

It does not promise sending, editing, reacting or live delivery, unread counts and push, group conversations between students, or staff tooling for reports.
