# Staff act under the company's identity, and every action leaves a trail

## Outcome

Only people with a LevApp company Google account get into the staff console, each with a role that says what they may do, and every change they make is recorded: who, what, the value before and after, and when. Closing the Google account ends access at once; there is no separate password to revoke.

- Staff sign in only with a company Google account; the console has no passwords.
- Three roles: the owner (everything, including giving and removing roles), operators (everything except roles) and support (read-only). There is always at least one owner.
- Every console change is recorded, and the record cannot be edited or deleted from the console.
- A staff sign-in never works in the coach or student apps, and a coach or student sign-in never works in the console.
- The coach and student apps, web and iPhone, hold no staff screens, actions or console code. The one exception is a read-only "viewing as" banner on the web app while a staff member looks at a user's app.
- The console runs alongside the product, with its own test copy beside the product's.

It does not promise Google sign-in for coaches or students, or staff access from a phone app.
