# Staff operate the platform without the database

## Outcome

Everything the team does to run the platform day to day is a screen in one staff console, so routine operations take a minute, need no database access, leave a record, and cannot be done by accident from the coach app.

- Approving or rejecting a coach has exactly the effect it has today: the same status change, email and push to the coach, and CRM update.
- The old Admin section in the coach app's Settings disappears from web and iPhone in the release that adds approvals to the console.
- Support staff see every page except the work page and change nothing outside finance. Only the owner gives or takes a staff role, and only the owner switches a feature off for everyone.
- Seeing a user's app "as them" is read-only, never shows their private messages, is recorded every time, and is for the operator role only.
- The engine health page only reads; nothing on it starts, stops or retries the engine.
- Plans and what they include are recorded before any payment exists; charging money is a later decision.
- Every console screen works at phone width in a phone's browser, with the same actions as at a desk.
- A person who asks for a copy of their data gets it within 30 days of LevApp confirming who they are: a read-only export of that one person's data, never another person's messages or any password or token (PAD-640).
- The owner sees on one page the tickets left to do (with a short summary), the tickets done and the changes shipped, and whether each change is on staging or live; nothing on it changes a ticket or pull request, and the Linear and GitHub keys stay on the server (PAD-555).
- A reported message reaches staff, who see it in context and dismiss it, delete it or disable its author; every decision is recorded, and the admin gets one email per burst of reports, with a link and no message text (PAD-631).

It does not promise charging coaches, editing a user's classes, players or evaluations on their behalf, or a phone app for the console.
