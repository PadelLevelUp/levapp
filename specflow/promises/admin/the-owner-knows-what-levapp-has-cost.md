# The owner knows what LevApp has cost

## Outcome

One console page shows every cost LevApp has had since it started, month by month and by category, with each month's total and the running total. Google Cloud figures arrive by themselves once a day; every other cost is entered once and repeats on its own when it is a subscription (PAD-742).

- Every amount is shown in euros; a cost charged in another currency keeps the charged amount, in that currency, beside the euro amount debited.
- The history starts at the month of the first cost entered.
- Every staff console account sees and changes the finance page.
- Every change to a cost is recorded with who and when, and the old value is kept in the finance history. The general activity log notes that a cost changed and which fields, never the amounts.
- Google Cloud billing data is only read, never changed.
- Google Cloud costs are counted once: a month has the invoice total or the imported figures, never both.
- Google Cloud credits are shown and taken off, so the month matches the invoice.

It does not promise revenue, plans or prices, payments, budgets or spending alerts, a phone app, or automatic import from any supplier other than Google Cloud.
