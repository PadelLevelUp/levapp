# Personal data is kept only as long as it is needed

## Outcome

LevApp removes personal data it no longer needs on a fixed schedule, and the Privacy Policy states how long each kind is kept (PAD-638).

- Invitations, join links and expired verification or reset codes go 30 days after they expire.
- Notification and reminder history goes after 12 months.
- Audit records of deletions and of staff actions go after 5 years, and nothing changes them before then.
- A student a coach added who never created an account is never deleted by the schedule; the coach removes them.
- A deleted message keeps its place in the conversation forever; its text is erased when it is deleted.
- Web server access logs rotate after about 14 days.

It does not promise any change to the web server's log rotation.
