# Coach builds their roster

## Outcome

A coach gets students onto their roster four ways and never ends up with two records for one person: fill in the basics themselves, hand a one-time invite link to complete a profile, show a QR code or link any student with an account can use to join, or link a record they created to an account the student made on their own, keeping what the coach entered. A name that may already be on the roster brings a warning, never a block.

- A coach adds only people 18 or over, and confirms it when adding a student (PAD-642).
- Every link and QR code shows the levapp.app address, whichever address the coach used; ones already shared on padellevelup.com keep working (PAD-595).
- A coach never sets a student's username; the student picks it on activation or profile completion.
- A new player starts as an inactive account until the student completes it.
- The duplicate-name check compares names case-insensitively and exactly, and only warns.
- A profile-completion invite link is single-use and expires after 7 days.
- A username chosen at completion is unique across the app.
- The level field always offers real options or a nudge to define some, never an empty control.
- A join QR or link is reusable and coach-scoped: one code for many students, for seven days, retired when the coach makes a new one. Redeeming needs a signed-in student and their confirmation; the coach is not asked again.
- Only a never-activated account (no password, placeholder username) can be claimed. Claiming folds the coach's record into the student's account and retires the placeholder: the student's name and username win, and the coach's level, side, notes and evaluations are kept, also when the student was already on that roster (PAD-528).
- A student with an account is reached, added or linked only by something they do: scan, open a link, accept a request. Merging a coach-created record into a student already on that coach's roster is the coach's own dedupe and needs nothing from the student.

It does not promise self-registration, editing or removing players, browsing the roster, or activation mechanics.
