# Coach blocks personal time

## Outcome

A coach marks out personal time on the calendar, visible beside their classes, and may have it suppress automatic class invitations to themself during that window.

- A block has a type: break, holiday, off-work, personal or unavailable.
- Blocks can recur, with the same weekly recurrence as classes.
- A block can suppress automatic invitations sent to its owner during its window.

It does not promise a student's own availability blocks.
