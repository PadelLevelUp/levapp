# A student on a phone finds the iOS app

## Outcome

A student who opens the web app on an iPhone learns once, without being blocked, that the iOS app exists and is the better way to use LevApp on a phone, and reaches its App Store listing in one tap.

- Students only; a coach never sees the suggestion (PAD-573).
- iPhone-class browsers only; desktop and Android browsers see nothing.
- Dismissing hides it for 30 days on that device; it never holds the student up.
- The text follows the app's language.
- It exists only on the web.
- Nothing is measured: the signed-in app has no analytics.

It does not promise an Android nudge or in-app analytics.
