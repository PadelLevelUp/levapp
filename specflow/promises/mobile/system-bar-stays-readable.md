# The phone's own status bar stays readable on every screen

## Outcome

On an iPhone, the clock, signal and battery at the top are always legible in LevApp: light over the dark navy headers, dark over light screens (PAD-419).

- The status bar always contrasts with what the app paints under it.
- It follows the screen: going back restores the previous screen's style.

It does not promise anything for the web app, whose browser draws its own bar, or for Android's status and navigation bar colours.
