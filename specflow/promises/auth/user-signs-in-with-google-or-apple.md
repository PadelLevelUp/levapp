# User signs in with Google or Apple

## Outcome

A coach or a student creates their account, and signs in afterwards, with their Google account on the web and the iPhone, or their Apple account on the iPhone. Someone who already has an account with the same email lands in that account, not a new one.

- The same checks as a password sign-up apply: adults only, country, the Terms accepted, coaches approved.
- An existing account is joined only when the provider has verified the email.
- LevApp receives the name, the email and an account id, never the password, and does not keep the profile photo.
- On the iPhone, Google and Apple are offered together or not at all (App Store rule 4.8).
- Each button appears only once its provider is set up; until then nothing changes for anyone.

It does not promise Android, Apple sign-in on the web, or linking a second provider from settings.
