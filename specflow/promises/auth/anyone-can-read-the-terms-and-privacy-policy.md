# Anyone can read the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy

## Outcome

A visitor, someone signing up, a signed-in user on the web or iOS, and an app-store reviewer can all open the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy at stable public addresses (`/terms`, `/privacy`), see which version is in force and since when, and read them in their language where a translation exists.

- The published text is the source of truth for what the product promises. The pages change only when the operator publishes a new version, and the version and effective date shown always match the text.
- New sign-ups record the versions in force; nobody is asked again when a version changes (PAD-601, PAD-603, PAD-634).
- English prevails; a Portuguese version is a courtesy translation.
- The pages are public, need no account, and are served by the web app so the store listings and the apps can link to them.
