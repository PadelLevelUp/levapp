# Coach schedules recurring classes

## Outcome

A coach sets up a one-off or recurring class, with its type, capacity and level, and later adjusts or cancels it, including one occurrence of a series, without disturbing the rest.

- A class is one-off or recurring; a recurring class repeats weekly on chosen days until an end date or the coach's season end.
- A class is an "academy" group class or a "private" one-on-one lesson.
- Editing or deleting always targets an explicit scope the coach chooses: one occurrence, or it and every future one.
- A recurring class stays one class through its edits: splitting it at a date, renaming it or removing one date never makes two unrelated classes in the coach's lists (PAD-275).
- Changing one occurrence never disturbs its siblings; only a future-scoped change ripples forward.
- Deleting a whole class removes every occurrence and any pending reminders for it.
- Picking start and end works the way the device works, on every browser and the phone (PAD-559): the time list scrolls with wheel or trackpad, a time can be typed or walked with the arrow keys or picked by finger, the end is suggested from the coach's usual class length and never lands before the start, and the list opens near now.

It does not promise who is enrolled or teaches, or how classes show on the calendar.
