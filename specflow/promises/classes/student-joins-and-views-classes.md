# Student joins and views classes

## Outcome

A student sees only their own information when looking at a class, and can ask to attend an open seat in a class they may join, with the coach deciding.

- A request to join an academy class appears with every other class request: in the coach's "Pedidos de Aula" to accept or decline, and in the student's own requests in Availability to follow or withdraw, marked as an academy class with name, date and time. It is accepted or declined, never answered with another time (PAD-460).
- A student's view of a class never includes another student's participation, attendance or invitation history.
- Only an eligible student who can see the open spot may request it; eligibility is re-checked when they ask and when the coach decides.
- A student cannot request a class they are enrolled in.
- The wizard's academy list never shows a class the student could not request or queue for: another coach's, one above their level, or one the coach keeps private.
- A student may join a full class's waiting list, which queues them and never enrols them; when a spot opens the engine asks them first, as an invitation.
- A student can withdraw their own pending request without alerting the coach.
- The coach decides each request. A coach may choose to let eligible requests in at once (PAD-672); nothing is ever refused automatically.
- First fill wins: whichever accepted request or invitation fills the spot first closes every other pending request for it.
- The coach is always told when a spot they had requests for is filled by someone else.

It does not promise how open spots become visible, or the invitation engine.
