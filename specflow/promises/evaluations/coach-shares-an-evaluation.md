# Coach shares an evaluation

## Outcome

A coach shows a player part of an evaluation on purpose, choosing exactly what to show and previewing what the player will see (PAD-402).

- Nothing is visible to a player until a coach shares it.
- The private note travels only when the coach ticks it.
- What the player sees is frozen at the moment of sharing.
- A share shows the trend of every competency the coach chose, over the period chosen, or no trend where there is nothing to compare.
- An empty evaluation cannot be shared.
- Sharing sends one in-app message and no push; updating or un-sharing sends nothing.
- Deleting an evaluation stops the player seeing it.
- Evaluations from before sharing existed can be shared.
- Each coach shares only their own evaluations.

It does not promise automatic sharing, or anything a player can do to an evaluation.
