# Student sees their evaluations

## Outcome

A player sees what their coach chose to show them, in one section of their dashboard on web and iOS, and can go back to it. A player with several coaches sees each coach's evaluations, named (PAD-402).

- A player sees only what was shared, exactly as previewed.
- A player sees nothing of an unshared evaluation, and no figure worked out from parts they were not shown.
- With nothing shared there is no evaluations section at all.
- A player cannot rate, reply to or remove an evaluation; the one action is opening the conversation with the coach.
- App Store versions from before this feature do not show the section and nothing breaks; the message still arrives.

It does not promise a player profile page, or anything not shared.
