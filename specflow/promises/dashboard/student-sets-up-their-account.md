# Student sets up their account

## Outcome

A new student is walked through the five things that decide whether LevApp reaches them: request alerts, notifications on this device, the hours they are unavailable, a link to a coach, and a coach-completed profile. They see how far along they are, and they can finish now, later, or never.

- The guide opens on the student's dashboard when they first sign in, on web, iOS and Android, and shows "N de 5 passos · X%". Each finished step is worth a fifth, so X is always the share of the five steps that are done.
- A student who joined through a coach's link starts with the coach step done.
- "Agora não" leaves a compact card on the dashboard, and the guide comes back the next day. "Não mostrar mais" removes it from the dashboard for good, and it stays reachable in Perfil › Configurar conta. Both choices hold on every device the student signs in on.
- Unavailable hours made in the guide are ordinary availability blocks. They hold back invitations and reminders and keep those hours out of class booking, exactly like blocks made on the Disponibilidade tab.
- The profile step is done only when every coach has set the student's level and side. The student can only remind the coach.
- When all five steps are done, a "Conta pronta" card appears once, and the guide leaves the dashboard.

It does not promise the behaviour of the screens the steps open: Settings, the system's notification settings, the coach-link flow.
