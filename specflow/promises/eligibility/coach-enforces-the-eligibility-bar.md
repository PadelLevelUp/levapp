# Coach enforces the eligibility bar

## Outcome

Once a coach sets an eligibility bar, it is a hard wall for everything the system does on its own: the automatic invitation engine and the waiting list stop considering students who do not clear it. The coach's own judgment is never overridden: adding a student by hand still works, with a warning naming why they fall short. Tightening the bar governs joining, never staying.

- Automatic invitation rounds and waiting-list invitations are hard-gated by the bar; a coach acting by hand is only warned, never blocked.
- The warning names every rule a student fails in concrete terms ("2 levels below this class", "4 unjustified absences, limit is 2"), never a bare "not eligible".
- One check decides pass or fail and writes the warning, so they never disagree.
- A student is never offered, from the waiting list, the spot their own cancellation just created, or a class they are already in.
- Waiting-list invitations, manual invitations and the automatic engine all honour the same exclusion and restriction settings (excluded players, inactive accounts, availability blocks), with or without a bar.
- Tightening a bar only reports which enrolled students would now fail it; it removes and notifies nobody.
- With no bar, automatic invitations stay as unfiltered as before.

It does not promise defining the bar, students discovering open spots, or the engine's matching order.
