# Student tracks attendance and absence history

## Outcome

A student looks back at the classes they attended and missed, as a chart and a list whose entries link to the class on the calendar; a coach sees the same two views for any player on their roster.

- The attended page counts only "present" classes and the missed page only "absent" ones: the same numbers as the dashboard's cards.
- The missed page counts every absence, justified or not; justification shows per row and never changes the total.
- A coach sees this only for a player on their roster; a student only their own.
- The chart has no gaps: every period in the range appears, even with zero classes.

It does not promise recording or finalizing attendance, or confirming or cancelling ahead of a class.
