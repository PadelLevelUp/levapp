# Student controls their notifications

## Outcome

A student who does not want to be asked about classes says so once, in their own Settings, at the level they need: skip automatic invites, skip manual invites, or skip everything including reminders. Their coach sees that they opted out, and why if they said. Direct messages, cancellation notices and "you got the spot" always still reach them (PAD-112).

- The three levels are independent and combine freely; the broadest never flips the narrower ones.
- A coach inviting a blocked student by hand creates no invitation; the student is shown as skipped, with their reason.
- Blocking everything also silences reminders and needs explicit confirmation, because an unconfirmed absence can count as unjustified.
- Direct messages, cancellation notices and spot confirmations are never affected.
- The reason is visible to the coach, unlike a calendar availability block.
- The preferences live only in the student's Settings.
- Waiting-list invitations are automatic invitations and follow these preferences; nobody is added from the waiting list without being asked (PAD-446).

It does not promise calendar availability blocks.
