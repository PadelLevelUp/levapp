# People hear about requests that wait for them

## Outcome

When a coach asks to join a club, a coach asks a student to link a placeholder account, or a self-registered coach awaits LevApp approval, the people who can decide are told at once, by push in the phone and browser and by email, and the requester is told the moment a decision lands.

- Alerts go only to those who can act (club members for a join request, the invited account for a claim, superadmins for a coach approval) and, on a decision, only to the requester.
- Anyone can switch these alerts off for their own account in Settings; badges and banners stay.
- Alerts are best-effort: a failed push or email never fails the request.
