# Coach understands who gets invited

## Outcome

From a Tutorials area in Settings, a coach picks an upcoming class and one of its players as "the one who cancels", and sees exactly who the engine would invite now, in what order and why: round by round, with the ranking values, and whether each is in the first batch. They can ask about any student not listed and get the one reason they were left out. Nothing is sent; it is a rehearsal.

- The rehearsal gives the engine's own answer, produced by the code that fills real spots, never a copy of it.
- It changes nothing: no invitation, message, approval request, enrolment, credit or open spot.
- It answers as of now, and says when it was evaluated.
- "Why not" names one reason, the first that stopped the student; for the eligibility bar, the failed rule in the manual-add warning's words.
- Only coaches see Tutorials.
- Web and iOS ship it together.

It does not promise rehearsing another moment, listing everyone not invited, running it from a class or a real vacancy, or other tutorials.
