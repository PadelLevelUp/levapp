# Coach approves replacements

## Outcome

A coach who wants a say in every replacement switches to semi-automatic mode: instead of invitations going out when a spot opens, the platform assistant shows them who dropped out and the exact order the engine would invite in, and they approve it (now, or when the invitation window opens) or decline and handle it by hand.

- It applies only when the engine is on and semi-automatic is chosen; fully automatic coaches never see a prompt.
- Going back to fully automatic hands over the spots still awaiting an answer: they start inviting at the coach's start time, within a couple of minutes if it has passed, and their old prompts stop deciding anything. An ignored spot stays ignored.
- Every prompt lives in the coach's assistant conversation as the permanent record.
- The students shown are exactly those the engine may invite; eligibility is re-checked at send time, so someone shown may be skipped and, rarely, someone not shown may qualify.
- When the engine can suggest nobody, the prompt says why in numbers: how many fall outside the rules, are excluded, are busy that day, and so on.
- A decision is final; a dismissed prompt cannot be revived.
- Saying no does not close the spot: the engine stays out, and manual invitations and the waiting list remain.
- Waiting-list students are asked like everyone else: nothing goes out to anyone before the coach approves.
- Every spot awaiting the coach's answer shows on the class, oldest first, listed as the class is now (PAD-765).
- A spot filled another way or run out stops asking at once, and its question says so (PAD-765).
- The class follows changes while open. The question says what time its list is from, and answering it never invites a student who joined or declined since, or more students than there are free spots (PAD-770).

It does not promise changes to the matching rounds, or the engine's timing and wording settings.
