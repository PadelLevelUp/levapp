# Student gets one clear invitation per class date

## Outcome

Part of the redesigned invitation engine (PAD-899). A student is invited to a class date once, the
invitation says only what they need to know, and it is never edited to "filled" and back. Their Yes
gets them a place whenever one they fit is still there.

- One invitation per student per class date, never one per spot.
- The message names a side only when it asks the student to play outside their own: a left-sider
  asked to play right is told "right side"; a student who plays both sides is never told a side.
- An invitation stays open until the student answers or the class starts. Nobody withdraws it when
  someone else takes a spot.
- On Yes, the student is placed on what the invitation offered. A side the message named binds them
  to it. A student who plays one side takes it when it is open, even from the waiting list; one who
  plays both takes the side that is hardest to fill.
- If what they were offered has filled but another place they fit is open, the same invitation asks
  them about that place ("the right side filled; can you play left?") instead of a new one.
- If nothing they fit is left: "sorry", with the offer to join the waiting list; if a place they fit
  opens later, the same invitation asks them again.
- No is final for invitations to that class date; a coach who allows direct joins still lets the
  student join a place on their own.
- Two students saying Yes to the last place at the same moment: exactly one gets it, the other is
  told "sorry".

It does not promise the reminder flow (P-NOTIFICATIONS-7) or how the engine chooses whom to ask
(P-NOTIFICATIONS-8).
