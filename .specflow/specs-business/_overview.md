# Business outcomes

## What this is

The business layer: user outcomes and journeys written for stakeholders, in plain language — one file per persona journey, grouped by domain.

## What it covers

33 outcomes across 15 domains, authored 2026-09-03 from the developer leaves (insight-first onboarding). Files named persona-first (`coach-…`, `student-…`, `user-…`). `status: implemented` means every developer leaf behind the outcome is implemented (Policy A); `draft` means at least one is still open. `OPEN:` notes mark grouping judgment calls awaiting the owner's review.

## Why it's grouped this way

One folder per business domain, mirroring the developer tree, so stakeholders find outcomes by area and every leaf's `implements:` link stays local to its domain.
