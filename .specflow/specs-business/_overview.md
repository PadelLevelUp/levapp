# Business outcomes

The business layer: user outcomes and journeys written for stakeholders, in plain language.

15 domains. Every file is currently a DRAFT placeholder generated from the legacy domain intro (2026-09-03); Phase 6 onboarding splits each into persona journeys.

## Why it's grouped this way

One folder per business domain, mirroring the developer tree, so stakeholders find outcomes by area.
