# training

## What this is

Business outcomes for the training domain: building an exercise library, and using it to plan a
specific class.

## What it covers

- `training.coach-builds-exercise-library` — exercises, groups, the court-diagram editor, and the
  library browsing/filtering view.
- `training.coach-plans-training-for-a-class` — attaching library exercises to a specific class
  occurrence.

## Why it's grouped this way

Split by outcome: building and maintaining the reusable exercise library is a different journey,
on a different cadence, from planning what a single upcoming class will cover — even though the
second depends entirely on the first.
