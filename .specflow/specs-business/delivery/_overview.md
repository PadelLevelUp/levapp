# delivery — Releases that are not held back

## What this is

Outcomes about how changes reach the people using the app: that a broken screen is caught early,
on the change that broke it, so releases are on time.

## What it covers

- The owner sees a broken screen on its change, not at the release.

## Why it's grouped this way

These outcomes are about the team's way of shipping, not about coaching or training, so they live
apart from the product domains.

## Related groups

- Mobile: the apps' own release and runtime outcomes.
