The unit of the "Por resolver" queue.

```jsx
<ActionCard
  accent="attention"
  title="Aula 12 tem 9 vagas"
  detail="quinta 6 ago · 10:30 · Nível 4- · 7/16 inscritos"
  primaryLabel="Convidar 12 jogadores"
  secondaryLabel="Depois"
/>
```

Every card must be resolvable from the card — if the coach has to navigate away to decide, it does not belong in the queue. The queue reaching zero is the design goal; never pad it with informational rows.
