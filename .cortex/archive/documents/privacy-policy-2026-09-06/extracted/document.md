# Privacy Policy
_Effective date: 6 September 2026_
This Privacy Policy explains how **Sucesso Fractal – Lda**, operator of Levapp ("Levapp", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use our mobile applications, websites, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Levapp provides tools for coaches and players to manage coaching activities, including schedules, attendance, evaluations, calendars, messaging, and related information.

For the personal data covered by this Privacy Policy, **Sucesso Fractal – Lda** is the data controller unless otherwise stated.

Our contact details are provided in Section 14 below.

## 1. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Levapp.

### Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- name;
- username;
- email address;
- password, stored only in hashed form and not in plain text;
- phone number, where you choose to provide one;
- profile photo, where you choose to provide one;
- whether the account is a coach or player account;
- information relating to the connection between coaches and players.

We do not currently use third-party or social sign-in. Accounts are created using Levapp's own account system.

### Coaching and activity information

When the Service is used to manage coaching activities, we may process information including:

- class and training schedules;
- calendar information;
- attendance records;
- class rosters;
- skill levels;
- evaluations;
- coaching notes;
- information entered by coaches relating to their players;
- information necessary to organize and manage coaching activities.

Some of this information may be entered about a player by their coach rather than directly by the player.

Coaches are responsible for ensuring that they are entitled to provide personal data about other people through the Service.

### Messages and communications

We process the content of messages sent through Levapp so that messages can be delivered to and viewed by the intended participants.

We may also process information associated with those communications, such as the sender, recipient, and time of the message.

### Safety and moderation information

If you report inappropriate behaviour, contact us about a safety issue, or if we investigate suspected misuse of the Service, we may process:

- the content of the report;
- relevant account information;
- relevant messages or other User Content;
- information about blocked users;
- communications between you and Levapp concerning the report.

### Parental consent information

Where parental consent is required, we may collect information necessary to obtain, record, and reasonably verify that consent.

This may include information such as:

- the parent or legal guardian's name;
- contact details;
- their relationship to the child;
- confirmation of the consent provided;
- the date and status of that consent;
- information reasonably necessary to verify parental responsibility where required.

We only use this information for managing parental consent, protecting minors, complying with applicable law, and related security purposes.

### Technical and security information

When you connect to Levapp, our systems and infrastructure may process limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the Service, such as:

- IP address;
- request and connection timestamps;
- basic server and security logs;
- information relating to authentication sessions;
- information necessary to detect errors, abuse, unauthorized access, or security incidents.

If push notifications are enabled, the Service may also process a notification token generated by your device platform for the purpose of delivering those notifications.

We do not currently use advertising SDKs, cross-app advertising identifiers such as Apple's IDFA, or third-party tracking for advertising purposes.

We do not currently use your activity in Levapp to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## 2. How we collect information

We collect personal data:

- directly from you when you create or update your account;
- when you use Levapp and its features;
- from coaches when they enter information about players they coach;
- from parents or legal guardians when parental consent is required;
- automatically from our systems where technically necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

We use personal data to:

- create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts;
- provide the features and functionality of Levapp;
- connect coaches with their players;
- organize classes and coaching activities;
- manage schedules and calendars;
- record and display attendance;
- record and display coaching evaluations and notes;
- deliver messages between authorized users;
- send service-related notifications and communications;
- provide customer support;
- operate parental consent procedures;
- investigate abuse, inappropriate behaviour, or safety reports;
- block or restrict abusive users where appropriate;
- protect users, including minors;
- prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
- maintain and troubleshoot the Service;
- comply with legal obligations;
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not use personal data to serve third-party advertising.

## 4. Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") applies, we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis for doing so.

Depending on the circumstances, those bases include:

### Providing the Service

We process information necessary to create your account and provide the Levapp features you request where this is necessary to perform our agreement with you or take steps at your request before providing the Service.

### Legitimate interests

We may process information where necessary for our legitimate interests or those of another person, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

These interests include:

- operating and improving the reliability of Levapp;
- protecting accounts and systems;
- preventing fraud and abuse;
- enforcing our Terms of Service;
- protecting users and minors;
- investigating safety incidents;
- maintaining appropriate records relating to the operation of the Service.

### Consent

We rely on consent where required by applicable law or where we specifically ask for it.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn.

### Legal obligations

We may process or retain personal data where necessary to comply with an obligation imposed by applicable law or a lawful request from a competent authority.

## 5. Children and parental consent

Levapp may be used by minors.

In Portugal, users aged **13 or older** may create and use a Levapp account without going through Levapp's formal parental consent process.

Users **under 13** may only activate and use their account after the required consent has been provided through our parental consent process by a parent or legal guardian.

Different age requirements may apply in other countries. Where applicable law requires parental consent at a higher age, we may apply that higher age.

Where parental consent is required:

- the account remains inactive until the required consent has been obtained;
- we may take reasonable steps to verify that consent has been provided by a person with parental responsibility;
- the parent or legal guardian may withdraw consent;
- the parent or legal guardian may request deletion of the child's account and exercise applicable data protection rights on the child's behalf.

Nothing in this section limits any additional protection provided to minors under applicable law.

For example:

- players may see information made available to them by their coach;
- messages are visible to the participants in the relevant conversation;
- schedules, attendance, and evaluations are visible to the users who are authorized to access them.

Users must not access or use another person's personal information for purposes unrelated to the Service or where they are not authorized to do so.

- investigate a user report;
- investigate suspected abuse or misuse;
- protect users, including minors;

## 10. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

The applicable retention period depends on the type of information and why we process it.

In general:

- account and profile information is retained while the account remains active;
- coaching, attendance, evaluation, schedule, and related records may be retained while they are needed to provide the Service and maintain relevant historical records;
- messages are retained while necessary to provide conversation history to authorized participants, subject to account deletion, applicable data-protection rights, and legal requirements;
- parental consent records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to demonstrate that the required consent was obtained;
- security and abuse-related records may be retained for a reasonable period where necessary to protect the Service, prevent repeated abuse, investigate incidents, or establish or defend legal claims;
- information that must be retained by law will be retained for the period required by that law.

When information is no longer required, we delete it or anonymize it so that it can no longer reasonably be linked to an identifiable person.

Limited copies may remain temporarily in backups until those backups are overwritten or deleted in the normal course of our backup procedures.

## 11. Deleting your account

You can delete your Levapp account directly from within the app:

**Settings → Account → Delete account**

Deleting your account:

- signs you out and invalidates active account sessions;
- removes or anonymizes account identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, and profile information, as applicable;
- removes your account from active coach/player relationships so that it can no longer be used as an active Levapp account;
- initiates deletion or anonymization of personal data associated with the account, subject to the limited retention circumstances described below.

Some information may be retained where necessary to:

- comply with applicable law;
- protect the security and integrity of the Service;
- investigate fraud, abuse, or serious safety incidents;
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
- preserve records that another user is legally entitled to retain.

Where limited historical records must remain, we aim to minimize the personal data retained and remove the deleted account's identifying information where reasonably possible.

Because messages, notes, or other User Content can themselves contain personal information, deletion or anonymization of an account does not automatically mean that every piece of content is anonymous merely because the account name has been removed.

If personal information remains in historical content and you want it deleted, you may contact us at **admin@levapp.app**. We will consider the request in accordance with applicable data-protection law and any rights or obligations that apply to the other people involved.
