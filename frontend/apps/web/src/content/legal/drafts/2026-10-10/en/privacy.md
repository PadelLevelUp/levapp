# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Sucesso Fractal – Lda**, operator of LevApp ("LevApp", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use our mobile applications, websites, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

LevApp provides tools for coaches and players to manage coaching activities, including schedules, attendance, evaluations, calendars, messaging, and related information. The Service is for adults only.

**Sucesso Fractal – Lda is the data controller for the whole platform.** Coaches are users of the Service and are not controllers of the personal data processed in it, and we have no data processing agreement with coaches. Our contact details are in Section 13.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account and profile information.** Name, username, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), phone number and profile photo where you choose to provide them, whether the account is a coach or player account, and the connection between coaches and players. We do not use third-party or social sign-in.

**Date of birth and country.** We collect your date of birth and country to check eligibility and to provide age-based features such as birthdays, age groups, and tournaments.

**Coaching and activity information.** Class and training schedules, calendar information, attendance records, class rosters, skill levels, evaluations, coaching notes, and other information needed to organise coaching activities. Some of this is entered about a player by their coach rather than by the player; see Section 3.

**Messages.** The content of messages sent through LevApp, and related information such as sender, recipient, and time.

**Safety and moderation information.** If you report inappropriate behaviour, contact us about a safety issue, or we investigate suspected misuse, we may process the report, relevant account information, relevant messages or other content, information about blocked users, and our communications with you about it.

**Technical and security information.** IP address, request and connection timestamps, basic server and security logs, authentication session information, and, if push notifications are enabled, a notification token generated by your device platform.

We collect this information directly from you, when you use LevApp, from coaches who enter information about their players, and automatically from our systems where technically necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

## 2. How we use your information and why

We use personal data to create and authenticate accounts; provide the features of LevApp (connecting coaches and players, schedules, attendance, evaluations, messages, notifications); check eligibility and provide age-based features; provide customer support; investigate abuse and safety reports; prevent fraud and security incidents; maintain and troubleshoot the Service; comply with legal obligations; and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. We do not use personal data to serve third-party advertising.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") applies, our lawful bases are:

- **performance of our agreement with you**, to create your account and provide the features you request;
- **legitimate interests**, such as operating and securing the Service, preventing fraud and abuse, enforcing our Terms of Service, investigating safety incidents, and keeping appropriate records, provided these are not overridden by your rights and freedoms;
- **consent**, where required by law or where we specifically ask for it (for example optional analytics cookies). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing;
- **legal obligations**, where we must process or retain data to comply with law or a lawful request from a competent authority.

## 3. Who can see your information

Some personal data is shared with other LevApp users as an inherent part of the Service. Coaches see information about the players they coach; players see information their coach makes available to them; messages are visible to the participants in the conversation; and schedules, attendance, and evaluations are visible to the users authorised to access them. Users must not access or use another person's personal information for purposes unrelated to the Service.

Coaches must be entitled to provide information about other people through the Service (see our Terms of Service, Section 2).

## 4. People without an account and legal representatives

If personal data about you has been entered in LevApp but you do not have an account, or if you are the legal representative of such a person (for example a parent), you can ask us for access, correction, or deletion by emailing **admin@levapp.app**. We may need to verify your identity and, for a representative, your authority to act.

## 5. Service providers and other recipients

We use trusted providers to help us operate LevApp. They process personal data only where necessary to provide their services to us and are required to protect it appropriately. The providers we declare are for:

- hosting;
- email delivery;
- push notifications;
- CRM;
- website analytics, with your consent where required;
- AI-assisted spreadsheet import, which sends limited information to an external AI provider.

When a coach creates an account, we record their name, email, phone (if given) and account status in our CRM (HubSpot, EU data centre) to follow up on their use of LevApp; we never share their students' data.

We may also disclose personal data where required by law or a valid request from a court, regulator, or other competent authority; where reasonably necessary to investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, threats, or serious safety risks; where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; and as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do **not** sell or rent users' personal data, and we do **not** share it with advertisers or data brokers for their own advertising purposes.

## 6. Messages, reports and moderation

Messages are stored so that the conversation can be provided to its participants. LevApp does not routinely read or pre-screen private conversations. We may access relevant messages or other information where reasonably necessary to investigate a user report or suspected abuse, protect users, investigate a security incident, enforce our Terms of Service, or comply with law or a lawful request from a competent authority, and only to the extent reasonably necessary for that purpose.

## 7. Cookies, local storage and tracking

LevApp uses cookies, local storage, and session storage that are needed for authentication and to remember your preferences. Optional analytics are used only with your consent.

The app itself contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and uses no device advertising identifiers (IDFA). On our public website we use HubSpot (HubSpot, Inc., EU data centre) to measure visits and to receive demo requests. Tracking cookies are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw that consent at any time under "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of the home page; with your consent, HubSpot receives the pages you visit, your IP address, browser details, the referring page and its own visitor cookie. Opening the demo request form loads it from HubSpot, which may set a short-lived security cookie on its own domain; what you submit in that form is stored in HubSpot.

## 8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this Privacy Policy. In general:

- account and profile information is kept while the account is active;
- coaching, attendance, evaluation, schedule, and related records may be kept while needed to provide the Service and maintain relevant historical records;
- messages are kept while necessary to provide conversation history to authorised participants, subject to account deletion, data-protection rights, and legal requirements;
- security and abuse-related records may be kept for a reasonable period to protect the Service, prevent repeated abuse, or establish or defend legal claims;
- information that must be retained by law is kept for the period required by that law.

When information is no longer required, we delete it or anonymise it so that it can no longer reasonably be linked to an identifiable person. Limited copies may remain temporarily in backups until they are overwritten in the normal course.

## 9. International data transfers

Our main infrastructure is located in the European Economic Area ("EEA"). Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we use Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or rely on a European Commission adequacy decision. You may contact us for more information about the safeguards for a particular transfer.

## 10. Deleting your account

You can delete your LevApp account directly in the app:

**Settings → Account → Delete account**

or by email to **admin@levapp.app**.

Deleting your account signs you out and invalidates active sessions; removes or anonymises identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, and profile information; removes you from active coach and player relationships; and starts deletion or anonymisation of the personal data associated with the account.

Some information may be retained where necessary to comply with law, protect the security and integrity of the Service, investigate fraud, abuse, or serious safety incidents, establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, or preserve records that another user is legally entitled to retain. Because messages, notes, and other content can themselves contain personal information, removing an account name does not automatically make all content anonymous. If personal information remains in historical content and you want it deleted, contact **admin@levapp.app** and we will consider the request in accordance with data-protection law and the rights of the other people involved.

## 11. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), password hashing, authentication and access controls, security monitoring, and restrictions on access to personal data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or personal information has been compromised, contact **admin@levapp.app**.

## 12. Your rights and complaints

Where the GDPR applies, you may have the right to:

- obtain confirmation that we process your personal data and request access to it;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
- request deletion of personal data;
- request restriction of processing;
- receive certain personal data in a portable format;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law. You can exercise them by contacting **admin@levapp.app**. We may need to verify your identity first. There is normally no charge, although the law allows fees or refusal for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data-protection authority:

**Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**

Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, Portugal

You may also complain to another competent supervisory authority. We encourage you to contact us first, but you are not required to.
